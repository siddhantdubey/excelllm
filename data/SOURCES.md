# Training data

`dinos.txt`: 2,053 dinosaur genus names (a–z, 4–15 letters) from the
[Paleobiology Database](https://paleobiodb.org), licensed CC BY 4.0. Downloaded 2026-11-25 from
`https://paleobiodb.org/data1.2/taxa/list.txt?base_name=Dinosauria^Aves&rank=genus&show=attr`.

The list keeps synonyms and dubious names (they still sound like dinosaurs). A few fossil birds and
trace-fossil genera (footprints, eggs) remain because PBDB files them under Dinosauria.
